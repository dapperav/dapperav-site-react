What we install

Dapper AV Solutions designs and installs UniFi business networks in Richmond, Rosenberg, Sugar Land, Fulshear, Stafford and Katy. Networks are planned at IT-director level: access points placed for your floor plan and the POS, cameras, staff and guests kept on separate networks, and the whole thing managed if you want.

  • UniFi access points placed and tuned for your floor plan
  • Switches, gateway and firewall set up properly
  • Separate networks (VLANs) for POS, cameras, staff and guests
  • Guest Wi-Fi that cannot reach business systems
  • Cabling to every access point
  • Remote monitoring and updates under a care plan

How much does business Wi-Fi installation cost?

It depends on the space. The biggest factors: square footage, and what the walls are made of; how many access points and switches it takes; firewall and network segmentation needs. Every proposal is itemized after a free 30–45 minute site survey, so you see what each piece costs before anything is installed.

Can you install without shutting us down?

Yes. Business installs are scheduled around your hours — before open, after close, or on weekends by appointment — so customers and staff never see a ladder.

Why should the POS be on its own network?

Card terminals sharing a network with guest phones is a security and reliability risk. Separate networks and firewall rules keep a busy guest network from slowing down or exposing your registers.

Who handles it when something stops working?

We do. The same company that installed it supports it. Every system can be covered by a month-to-month care plan with monitoring and support from 9 to 5 Central, plus emergency help after hours at an emergency rate. Or request service as needed.
