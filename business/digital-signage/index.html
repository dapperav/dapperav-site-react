Digital signage & menu boards

What we install

A printed menu board is out of date the day a price changes. Digital signage lets you change prices, run specials, and swap the lunch menu for dinner on a schedule — from your phone, without a ladder. We install the screens, the players and the network, and set it up so your staff can actually run it.

  • Digital menu boards for counters and drive-thrus
  • Lobby, waiting-room and wayfinding displays
  • Content updated remotely from a phone or computer
  • Day-part scheduling — breakfast, lunch, dinner, happy hour
  • Screens that power on and off with your hours
  • Reliable wired or Wi-Fi connections for every player

Why one company

The survey, the install and the support come from Dapper AV, never a subcontractor.

The network is half the job

Signage fails when the player loses its connection. Because we build networks too, the screens get a connection that doesn't drop.

Your staff runs it

We set up templates and show your team how to change a price or a special in minutes, so you are not paying someone every time the menu changes.

Clean, level, commercial-grade mounts

Rows of screens mounted dead level and evenly spaced, with power and data hidden — the same standard as every other install.

What the free survey checks

These are also what move the price. You get them itemized, line by line, before anything is installed.

  • How many screens and media players
  • Indoor screens, or outdoor-rated and drive-thru displays
  • The signage software subscription
  • Whether new power and data runs are needed at each screen

How much does digital signage or a digital menu board cost?

It depends on the space. The biggest factors: how many screens and media players; indoor screens, or outdoor-rated and drive-thru displays; the signage software subscription. Every proposal is itemized after a free 30–45 minute site survey, so you see what each piece costs before anything is installed.

Can you install without shutting us down?

Yes. Business installs are scheduled around your hours — before open, after close, or on weekends by appointment — so customers and staff never see a ladder.

Can I change prices myself?

Yes. Content is managed from a web dashboard, so a price or a special can change from your phone and show up on the screens in minutes.

Who handles it when something stops working?

We do. The same company that installed it supports it. Every system can be covered by a month-to-month care plan with monitoring and support from 9 to 5 Central, plus emergency help after hours at an emergency rate. Or request service as needed.
