Who we work with

Six kinds of business, each with its own page: what we install there, what moves the price and what owners ask first.

  • Restaurants & bars Multi-TV walls with a video matrix — any source on any screen. Zoned background music: bar, dining room, patio, restrooms.
  • Offices & conference rooms Conference room displays and video bars for Teams and Zoom. Wireless screen sharing — no cable hunt.
  • Retail & showrooms Showroom and feature-wall displays with concealed wiring. Product and promo screens on a schedule.
  • Digital signage & menu boards Digital menu boards for counters and drive-thrus. Lobby, waiting-room and wayfinding displays.
  • Business Wi‑Fi & networks UniFi access points placed and tuned for your floor plan. Switches, gateway and firewall set up properly.
  • Structured cabling & low voltage Cat6 data drops, jacks and patch panels. Network racks with clean cable management.

How a business job goes

Scheduled around your hours: before open, after close or on a weekend.

  1. 1

    Send the request form

    A few lines about the space. You hear back within one business day.

  2. 2

    Free site survey

    30–45 minutes on site. We walk the space and note every screen, drop and camera angle.

  3. 3

    Itemized proposal

    Equipment options at honest prices, every line priced before anything is installed.

  4. 4

    Install, then support

    Before open, after close or on weekends. Afterward the same company looks after it.

Documented, not just installed

Every job ends with a handover, so the next person, or us, can find and fix anything fast.

  • Every cable and port labeled
  • Separate networks for the POS, cameras, staff and guests
  • Notes on what went where, handed over at closeout
