Questions

Straight answers, up front

What business owners ask before a site survey.

How much do retail and showroom displays cost? +

It depends on the space. The biggest factors: how many displays, and whether they mount flush, recessed or standard; whether screens need scheduled content; how many background-music zones. Every proposal is itemized after a free 30–45 minute site survey, so you see what each piece costs before anything is installed.

Can you install without shutting us down? +

Yes. Business installs are scheduled around your hours — before open, after close, or on weekends by appointment — so customers and staff never see a ladder.

Can screens show different content at different times? +

Yes. Displays can run scheduled content — promotions during the day, something else after hours — and turn themselves on and off with your store hours.

Who do we call when something stops working? +

Me — the same person who installed it. Every system can be covered by a month-to-month care plan with monitoring and priority response, or you can call for service as needed.
