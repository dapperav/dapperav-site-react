What we install

A meeting that starts ten minutes late because nobody can get the screen to work costs more than the screen did. We build conference rooms where you walk in, share, and start — and back them with an office network and Microsoft 365 setup managed by someone with IT-director experience.

  • Conference room displays and video bars for Teams and Zoom
  • Wireless screen sharing — no cable hunt
  • Ceiling microphones and speakers for larger rooms
  • Office Wi-Fi and structured cabling to every desk
  • Microsoft 365 setup, users, licensing and email security
  • Network rack, cabling and documentation for the server closet

How much does a conference room AV setup cost?

It depends on the space. The biggest factors: room size and how many people it seats; display size, and whether you need a video bar or ceiling microphones; teams or Zoom room licensing. Every proposal is itemized after a free 30–45 minute site survey, so you see what each piece costs before anything is installed.

Can you install without shutting us down?

Yes. Business installs are scheduled around your hours — before open, after close, or on weekends by appointment — so customers and staff never see a ladder.

Do you work with Microsoft Teams and Zoom?

Yes. Rooms are set up for whichever platform you use — including Microsoft 365 accounts, room licensing, and the network settings that keep calls clear.

Can you take over IT for a small office?

Yes — that's what the care plans on our business page are for: network, cameras and Microsoft 365 managed month to month by the company that installed it.

Who handles it when something stops working?

We do. The same company that installed it supports it. Every system can be covered by a month-to-month care plan with monitoring and support from 9 to 5 Central, plus emergency help after hours at an emergency rate. Or request service as needed.
