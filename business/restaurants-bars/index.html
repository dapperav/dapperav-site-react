Restaurants & bars

TV walls where any screen shows any game, zoned music and a POS-safe network for Fort Bend County restaurants and bars. Installed after hours. Afterward, the same company supports it, not a ticket line.

What the free survey checks

  • How many TVs, and how big
  • Whether you need a video matrix so any source can go to any screen
  • Wall types and how far cable has to run
  • How many music zones, and whether the patio needs outdoor speakers
  • How much network work the POS, cameras and guest Wi-Fi need

These are also what move the price. You get them itemized, line by line, before anything is installed.

What we install

Game day is your busiest day, and that's exactly when a dead TV or a dropped card reader costs you the most. We design the whole room — TVs, sound, and the network underneath — so every seat sees the game, the patio plays its own music, and the POS never shares a network with the guest Wi-Fi.

  • Multi-TV walls with a video matrix — any source on any screen
  • Zoned background music: bar, dining room, patio, restrooms
  • Outdoor-rated TVs and speakers for patios
  • POS, cameras and guest Wi-Fi on separate, secured networks
  • UniFi Protect cameras for the register, bar and back door
  • One simple control for staff — no remote-control roulette

Why one company

  • One vendor for the room and the network

    The TVs, the music and the network all come from one company, so there's no finger-pointing between the AV company and the IT company when something breaks on a Saturday.

  • Built for staff, not technicians

    Your bartender should change a channel in two taps. Controls are set up and labeled for whoever is on shift.

  • Installed around service

    Work happens before open or after close. You open the next day with a finished room.

How much does a restaurant or bar TV and audio install cost?

It depends on the space. The biggest factors: how many TVs, and how big; whether you need a video matrix so any source can go to any screen; wall types and how far cable has to run. Every proposal is itemized after a free 30–45 minute site survey, so you see what each piece costs before anything is installed.

Can you install without shutting us down?

Yes. Business installs are scheduled around your hours — before open, after close, or on weekends by appointment — so customers and staff never see a ladder.

Can different TVs show different games?

Yes. With a video matrix any cable box or streaming source can go to any TV or group of TVs, and staff switch them from a tablet or wall panel instead of hunting for the right remote.

Why should the POS be on its own network?

Card terminals sharing a network with guest phones is a security and reliability risk. We separate POS, cameras, staff and guest traffic with VLANs and firewall rules so a busy guest network can't slow down or expose your registers.

Who handles it when something stops working?

We do. The same company that installed it supports it. Every system can be covered by a month-to-month care plan with monitoring and support from 9 to 5 Central, plus emergency help after hours at an emergency rate. Or request service as needed.
