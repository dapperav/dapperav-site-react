Privacy policy What we collect, and why.

Last updated November 19, 2026.

Privacy

The short version

The details are below. Nothing here changes without a new date at the top.

  • Your details are used only to answer your request.
  • We never sell them or share them with marketers.
  • Analytics counts visits and never records your name or contact details.
  • Ask, and we delete your request.

What you send us

When you use the request form we receive what you type into it: your name, a phone number or email, an optional business name, the service you picked, and any details you add. The form is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, a form-delivery service. We use these details only to answer your request, schedule a site survey and send your quote.

What we don't do

We don't sell your details, share them with marketers or add you to a newsletter. We don't send marketing email unless you ask for it.

What the site measures

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful, and it records when a request is sent (without your name or contact details). Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser for this. You can block or delete them in your browser settings, and the site works the same without them.

How long we keep it

Requests stay in our email for as long as they're useful for the job, its warranty and its support. If you'd like yours deleted, email contact@dapperav.com and we will.
