How a job goes

Survey to support, one person

  1. Step 1

    Free site survey

    30–45 minutes on site. I look at the room, the walls, the wiring and the network before anyone quotes anything.

  2. Step 2

    Itemized quote

    A written price, line by line. The number you get is the number you pay, with no add-ons on install day.

  3. Step 3

    Installed around your hours

    Before open, after close, or weekends by appointment. You open the next day with a finished room.

  4. Step 4

    Labeled, documented, supported

    Every cable labeled, a walkthrough at closeout, and the same person answers when something needs a look.
