What does the survey check in your space?

Pick the kind of space. These are the things that decide the scope and the price.

Kind of space

In a restaurant or bar, I check

  1. How many TVs, and how big
  2. Whether you need a video matrix so any source can go to any screen
  3. Wall types and how far cable has to run
  4. How many music zones, and whether the patio needs outdoor speakers
  5. How much network work the POS, cameras and guest Wi-Fi need

In an office, I check

  1. Room size and how many people it seats
  2. Display size, and whether you need a video bar or ceiling microphones
  3. Teams or Zoom room licensing
  4. How many desks need cabling or Wi-Fi coverage
  5. Whether Microsoft 365 needs a fresh setup or a migration

In a shop or showroom, I check

  1. How many displays, and whether they mount flush, recessed or standard
  2. Whether screens need scheduled content
  3. How many background-music zones
  4. Square footage the Wi-Fi has to cover
  5. How many cameras, and how many days of recording you want

For signage and menu boards, I check

  1. How many screens and media players
  2. Indoor screens, or outdoor-rated and drive-thru displays
  3. The signage software subscription
  4. Whether new power and data runs are needed at each screen

For Wi-Fi and the network, I check

  1. Square footage, and what the walls are made of
  2. How many access points and switches it takes
  3. Firewall and network segmentation needs
  4. Whether cabling already reaches the access point locations
  5. Whether you want ongoing management under a care plan

On a build or remodel, I check

  1. How many drops, and how far each run is
  2. Open framing, or finished walls and ceilings
  3. Rack, patch panel and cable management needs
  4. How many floors, and how the runs get between them
  5. How the work is phased around the build schedule

At home, I check

  1. TV size and the wall: drywall, brick, stone or tile
  2. Whether the cables can run inside the wall
  3. Whether an outlet needs to move behind the TV
  4. Where speakers and a soundbar or subwoofer will sit
  5. How the Wi-Fi reaches the rooms that matter
